Privacy & compliance

Only the tools you allow.

Remora has no server, no account and no telemetry. It talks directly to the tools you connect, and to nothing else. Built for organizations with strict data rules.

On the network

What leaves your Mac

Read requests to your own tools, with your own tokens. Data comes back and stays on your Mac.

Enforced, not promised

Checked in code, covered by tests.

Declared destinations

Every plugin declares the hosts it may reach. A test fails if one doesn’t.

One gate

Plugins only get a network client limited to their own hosts. Anything else fails with “Blocked: not an allowed destination”, look-alike domains included.

External AI off by default

Claude plugins are refused until external AI is allowed; then the brief, summaries and triage appear.

On-device intelligence

Sorting messages, topic similarity, ranking and snooze advice use Apple’s on-device language models. Nothing is sent.

No telemetry

No analytics, no crash reporting, no update pings. Fonts and logos are bundled, never downloaded.

Erase local data

Settings → Privacy lists every connected account’s destinations, and erases everything Remora stored in one click.

For IT

Lock the policy with MDM.

Deploy a configuration profile for the preference domain fr.igitscor.remora. Settings → Privacy then shows “Managed by your organization”, and users can’t change it.
