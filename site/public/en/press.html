Descriptions

One line

Everything that needs you, from every tool, in one inbox in your menu bar or tray.

30 words

Remora gathers code reviews, merge requests, Slack mentions and tasks from Linear, Jira, Notion and Microsoft 365 in one inbox, sorted by what to do. Free, open source, on your computer.

100 words

Remora is a free, open-source inbox for the work that waits on you. It lives in the menu bar on a Mac and in the tray on Windows and Linux, and gathers code reviews and merge requests from GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket and Azure DevOps, mentions and messages from Slack and Microsoft 365, and tasks from Linear, Jira and Notion. Instead of one list per tool, it sorts everything by what to do: to reply, to review, to fix. Remora has no server and no account: it talks to each tool from your computer and sorts there, and IT can lock its policy with MDM.
